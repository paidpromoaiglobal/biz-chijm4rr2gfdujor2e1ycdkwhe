About Us

B Unique Unisex Salon

B Unique Unisex Salon is Karapakkam's go-to destination for men, women, and everyone in between who wants to look and feel their absolute best. Nestled in the heart of the community near Anna Street, our passionate team of skilled stylists — including the much-loved Jenifer — brings creativity, precision, and genuine care to every appointment. From trendy wolf cuts and stunning balayage colour to relaxing manicures and pedicures, we do it all under one roof with a smile.

With a stellar 4.7-star rating backed by nearly 300 happy customers, we have built our reputation on delivering salon-quality results at affordable prices. Our doors are open seven days a week from 8:30 AM to 10:00 PM, so you can always find time for a little self-care. Whether you walk in with a clear vision or just a desire to look great, our stylists will listen, advise, and transform — leaving you absolutely in love with the results.
